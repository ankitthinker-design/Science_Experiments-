What is a lever?

A lever is a stiff bar that turns around a fixed point. It helps us move heavy things with a small push. People have used levers for thousands of years to lift rocks, build pyramids and open tins.

The secret rule

A door is a lever too! The hinge is the fulcrum. This door is stiff: it needs a turning effect of 30 to open. You push with 10 N. Tap a spot to push there.

Which spot do you think will work?
Turning effect = force × distance The distance is measured from the fulcrum. The same push turns things more when it is farther away. Balanced: both sides have the same turning effect, so nothing turns.
Unbalanced: one side is bigger, so the lever turns that way.

That is why door handles are on the side far from the hinges, and why spanners have long handles.

Three kinds of lever

Every lever has a fulcrum, a load and an effort. What changes is which one is in the middle.
